E&A Resources is a Nigerian enterprise focused on building valuable businesses and opportunities across real estate, energy, skills development, agriculture and other strategic areas.

WHO WE ARE

A Nigerian company built for value.

E&A Resources was established in 2023 with a vision to develop businesses that create lasting commercial and developmental value.

The company operates with an entrepreneurial and engineering-informed approach, pursuing opportunities that can contribute meaningfully to customers, communities, partners and the wider Nigerian economy.

LEADERSHIP

Engr. Edache James Adakole

Edache James Adakole is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of E&A Resources.

He studied Chemical Engineering at the University of Abuja and gained professional experience with Manzuwa Ventures before establishing E&A Resources in 2023.

As Founder and CEO, he provides leadership and strategic direction as the company develops its interests, builds partnerships and pursues opportunities across Nigeria.
