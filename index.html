FOUNDER & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Edache James Adakole.

Building an enterprise grounded in engineering, opportunity and long-term value.

Edache James Adakole is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of E&A Resources, a Nigerian enterprise established in 2023 with interests in Real Estate, Petroleum and related business opportunities.

He studied Chemical Engineering at the University of Abuja. Before establishing E&A Resources, he gained professional experience with Manzuwa and 363 Logistics, building practical exposure across business and operations.

As Founder and CEO, he provides leadership and strategic direction as E&A Resources develops its interests, builds partnerships and pursues opportunities across Nigeria.
