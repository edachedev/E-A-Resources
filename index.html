More About E&A Resources

E&A Resources Limited is the parent company of a diversified group with interests spanning multiple sectors. It oversees a portfolio of subsidiaries that include Real Estate (through E&A Homes), Downstream and Refining (through E&A Stations), Lakubus Cement, Agricultural and Food, Lakubus Air, Lakubus Foundation, Lakubus Tyre, Lakubus Hire, and Lakubus Academy (which includes Vocational Skill).

As the parent company, E&A Resources Limited provides strategic direction, governance, and coordination across these businesses, supporting their growth while maintaining a broad footprint in real estate, energy, manufacturing, agriculture, aviation, social development, and skills training

Through our businesses and initiatives, we aim to create value for customers, partners, investors, communities and other stakeholders.
